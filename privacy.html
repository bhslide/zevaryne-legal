1. Scope

This policy describes how Zevaryne Short Studio, a Windows desktop application for preparing short-form videos, handles information when you use the application and connect third-party platforms.

2. Information stored locally

The application stores its settings, processing history, analysis reports, review queue, and related metadata locally on your computer. Video source files and exported clips are normally read from and written to folders you configure. The application is designed primarily for local processing; your source videos are not automatically uploaded to the developer by the application.

3. Social account authorization

If you connect TikTok or YouTube, the application uses the relevant platform's authorization flow. Access and refresh tokens are stored using the operating system credential store through the Python keyring library, rather than in the ordinary application settings file. Client identifiers are stored in local settings; client secrets are supplied through Windows environment variables. Protect your Windows account and device.

The application uses account information and authorization tokens only to support the connected-account and publishing workflows. You can disconnect a platform in the application to remove its locally stored token. You can also revoke access through the relevant platform, where available.

4. Publishing and third-party recipients

When you request a publication, the selected video and the associated publishing metadata are sent to the platform you selected (for example, TikTok or YouTube) so it can process the request. The platform handles that information under its own privacy policy. Publication status and related API responses may be stored locally in the application.

5. Optional AI and transcription features

Video analysis, audio measurements, and transcription can run locally, depending on the features you enable and the software installed. Local Ollama-based suggestions are processed on your computer. If you configure an external AI API, selected metadata and text or transcript excerpts may be sent to that provider for analysis. Do not enable external AI processing for sensitive content unless you accept that transfer. Review the relevant provider's privacy policy and settings.

6. Diagnostics and network requests

The application makes network requests needed for platform authorization, token exchange, publishing, optional AI services, and software or model downloads initiated by you. Application errors may be written to local log files. This policy does not claim that third-party services collect no data; their own practices apply when you use them.

7. Legal basis and retention

Application data is primarily stored locally on your device. When Zevaryne receives a privacy request by email, the information in that request will be used to respond to it. Where applicable, you may exercise rights under the GDPR, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability, subject to legal conditions and exceptions. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL). The applicable legal basis and any retention period depend on the specific processing activity and should be assessed in context.

8. Security

Token storage relies on the operating system's credential store. No local storage method can guarantee absolute security. Keep your operating system updated, protect your account, and do not share your configuration files, logs, access tokens, or client secrets publicly.

9. Your choices and rights

You can stop using the application, disconnect connected accounts, disable optional AI features, and delete local application data. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of personal data. Requests relating to information held by TikTok, YouTube, or an AI provider should generally be directed to that provider.

10. Children

The application is not intended to be used in a manner that violates applicable age restrictions or the terms of connected platforms.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
